Per-attempt judge scores. Box plots show distribution per scenario; timelines show whether repeats cluster or scatter; cross-mutation comparison plots mean ± 95% CI so you can eyeball whether two mutations are statistically distinguishable (CIs overlap = not).

Pick 1 mutation to see distribution; pick 2-4 to compare side by side. Drop scenario filter to see all; pick one for a per-attempt timeline.

distribution per scenario · score

Wide box / long whiskers = high variance = scenario is noisy and a single repeat can't be trusted. Tight box = score is stable. Each colour = one mutation.

per-attempt timeline ·

Each dot is one judge verdict in chronological order. Tight cluster across repeats = stable; jumpy line = the scenario flaps and you need more repeats to trust the mean.

mutation comparison · mean ± 95% CI

Error bars are mean ± 1.96·SE (95% CI). When the bars overlap, the mutations aren't statistically distinguishable on this scenario at the current sample size — you need either more repeats or a larger effect.
